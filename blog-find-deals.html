How to Find the Best Deals Online Without Wasting Time: 8 Proven Methods

By Akshay · June 2026 · 5 min read
Home › Guides › How to Find the Best Deals Online

Stop Scrolling. Start Hunting Smart.

Most people think finding a good deal means spending hours browsing sale pages. It doesn't. The best deal hunters are efficient — they use a handful of reliable methods to spot real discounts in minutes, not hours. Here are the 8 methods that actually work, based on how I personally hunt deals every day for Dealbuster.

Method 1: Use a Price Tracker

The single most powerful tool for deal hunting. Price trackers show you the full price history of a product so you know whether a "discount" is real or manufactured.

  • Keepa — works for Amazon globally, including Amazon.in. Shows the complete price graph over months or years.
  • CamelCamelCamel — another Amazon tracker with email alerts when a product hits your target price.
  • PriceSpy India — tracks prices across multiple Indian retailers simultaneously.
Real talk: During the Amazon Great Indian Festival, roughly 30–40% of "deals" are on items whose prices were artificially inflated before the sale. Price trackers expose this instantly.

Method 2: Bookmark Deal Sites (Like This One)

Deal aggregator sites like Dealbuster do the hunting for you. We go through hundreds of products daily and only post the ones where the price drop is genuine. Instead of browsing Amazon for an hour, spend 5 minutes on a curated deal page. This is the biggest time saver in the list.

Method 3: Set Price Drop Alerts

Both Amazon and Flipkart allow you to add items to a wishlist and get notified on price drops. Combine this with a third-party tracker like Keepa and you'll get an alert the moment a product hits your target price — without having to check daily.

Method 4: Shop During the Right Windows

In India, the biggest sale windows are predictable every year:

  • January: Republic Day sales — good for electronics and appliances
  • March–April: End of financial year sales — random but genuine clearance
  • July–August: Independence Day sales and Prime Day
  • October: Great Indian Festival (Amazon) + Big Billion Days (Flipkart) — the biggest of the year
  • November: Black Friday + Singles Day — growing rapidly in India

If you're not in a rush, buying during these windows typically saves 20–50% on most categories.

Method 5: Compare Across Platforms

Never buy from the first site you check. The same product often has a price gap of ₹500–₹2,000 between Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, and brand-owned websites. Use Google Shopping (search the product → click "Shopping" tab) for a quick multi-platform price check.

Method 6: Check Bank and Card Offers

This is one the most overlooked deal source. Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra run ongoing bank card offers that give 5–10% instant discounts or cashback on top of sale prices. HDFC, SBI, and Axis Bank are the most common partners. Always check the "offers" section on the product page before checking out.

Stacking deals: Sale price + bank card offer + coupon code = the lowest possible price. This combination works more often than people realise.

Method 7: Buy Renewed / Open-Box for Electronics

Amazon Renewed and Flipkart's refurbished listings offer grade-A (essentially unused) electronics at 20–40% below new prices. These come with a 6-month replacement guarantee. For items like earbuds, smartwatches, and Kindle readers — this is genuinely the best value play.

Method 8: Wait 48 Hours on Impulse Buys

This isn't just willpower advice — it's a practical deal-finding technique. If you add something to your cart on Amazon or Flipkart and don't buy immediately, both platforms sometimes send you a coupon or a price-drop notification to get you to complete the purchase. This works surprisingly often on apparel and accessories.

The One Thing That Wastes the Most Time

Aimlessly browsing sale pages. You'll spend an hour and feel like you found deals — but most of them were marginal discounts on things you didn't need. Have a list. Use a tracker. Set alerts. Let the deals come to you instead of hunting blindly.

Final Word

Finding great deals online is a skill, not luck. The people who consistently pay the best prices use 2–3 of these methods routinely — not all eight every time. Start with a price tracker and a curated deal site. That alone will save you more money than any other single change you can make to how you shop online.
