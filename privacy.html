Privacy in Brief

Dealbuster is built around privacy-by-design. We do not require accounts, logins, or passwords to browse deals. We never sell your personal data, track your exact location, or harvest personal contacts.

What We Collect
  • ✓ Basic device & browser telemetry (screen size, OS, regional IP) to render deals smoothly.
  • ✓ Anonymous pageview counts via standard analytics tools.
  • ✓ Push notification tokens (only if you explicitly tap "Allow" for deal alerts).
  • ✓ Your email address only if you voluntarily contact our support.
What We NEVER Collect
  • ✕ No account registration, logins, or passwords.
  • ✕ No personally identifiable information (PII) like real names or phone numbers.
  • ✕ No precise GPS location tracking or Bluetooth scanning.
  • ✕ No access to device contacts, photos, or personal files.
01

Overview & Philosophy

Your privacy matters deeply to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Dealbuster ("we", "us", or "our") collects across our website (dealbuster.in), mobile applications, and community channels, how that information is handled, and the strict boundaries we enforce to protect you.

By using Dealbuster, you acknowledge the minimal data handling described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

We believe in collecting only the bare minimum data required to keep the service fast, secure, and helpful:

  • Usage & Technical Data: High-level IP region, browser type, operating system, and pages viewed — collected automatically via privacy-compliant analytics tools to detect service outages and improve performance.
  • Direct Communications: If you send us an email at contactdealbuster@gmail.com, we retain your email address and message contents solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Push Notifications: Push tokens are collected on the web or mobile app only when you explicitly opt in to deal drop alerts. You can disable notifications anytime in device settings.
03

Dealbuster Mobile Application Privacy Guarantee

📱

Zero Registration & Zero PII Architecture

The Dealbuster mobile application does not require any account registration, login, or personal profile, and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, contact lists, or location data.

The app does not request permissions to access your camera, microphone, photos, storage, contacts, or background location. It exists purely to stream deals to your screen with zero friction.

04

Analytics & Cookies

Analytics: We use industry-standard analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic trends — for example, which deal categories are most popular. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

Cookies & Storage: We use lightweight browser cookies or local storage strictly for functional purposes: remembering your display preferences, search cache, and maintaining affiliate link session continuity so qualifying purchases are properly credited. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to browse deals.

05

Third-Party Links & Merchants

Dealbuster contains links to external retailers (e.g., Amazon.in, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, TataCliq). Once you click an outbound deal link and navigate to a merchant's store, their own privacy policy, terms, and cookie practices govern your visit.

We do not share any personal user records with these merchants. We encourage you to review each retailer's privacy policy before completing a checkout.

06

Data Retention & Your Legal Rights

We retain support emails and aggregate analytics only for as long as necessary to maintain service stability and respond to inquiries.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) and applicable international standards, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold any personal data about you.
  • Request correction or complete erasure of any correspondence you have sent to us.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications or push notifications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our data controller at contactdealbuster@gmail.com.

07

Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Dealbuster is a general-audience shopping guide and does not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has shared personal data with us, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect product enhancements or legal updates. Material revisions will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document.
