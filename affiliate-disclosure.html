Overview

Dealbuster participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click certain links across our website, mobile application, or community channels and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn an affiliate commission from the retailer.

The price you see on the retailer's checkout page is the exact price you pay — clicking through Dealbuster will never increase the cost. In many cases, our automated alerts highlight coupons, bank discounts, or lightning deals that save you substantial money.

03

Editorial Independence

Our affiliate partnerships have zero impact on what we feature. Our automated crawlers and human curators monitor millions of products to find authentic price drops, price errors, and historical lows.

We do not accept paid placements, sponsored reviews, or merchant kickbacks to promote overpriced or poor-quality items. If an item drops to a poor rating or the price jumps back up, our system automatically marks it or removes it.
