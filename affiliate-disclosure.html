Overview

Dealbuster participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click certain links on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer — at no additional cost to you. The price you see is the price you pay. Retailers set their own prices; we have no influence over them.

Amazon Associates

Dealbuster is a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases made through our links.

Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial decisions. Deals are selected and featured based on genuine value and relevance to our readers — not commission rates. If we find an error or a deal that is no longer valid, we correct or remove it promptly.

Other Retailers

In addition to Amazon, we may use affiliate or tracking links for other merchants from time to time. Each affiliate programme operates under its own terms and conditions. We only partner with retailers we trust and that offer genuine value to our users.

Where You'll See Disclosures

Affiliate disclosures appear in the footer of every page on Dealbuster. You may also see reminders near deal listings and product content. We believe in full transparency — you should always know when a link may earn us a commission.
