Hey, I'm the founder.

Just a regular guy from India who got tired of fake discounts and decided to do something about it.

Why I built Dealbuster

I've always been obsessed with finding the best deal before buying anything. But every deal site I visited was the same story — inflated MRPs, so-called "80% off" on items that never actually sold at that price, and pages stuffed with ads rather than useful information.

So early in 2026, I built Dealbuster. Not as a big media project or a startup pitch — just as something I wished existed. A simple, honest site where every single deal has been looked at by me personally before it goes up.

No algorithms deciding what gets featured. No brand partnerships influencing the picks. Just me, going through product pages, checking real price history, and posting the ones that are genuinely worth your money.

If it's on Dealbuster, I'd actually consider buying it myself. That's the only filter that matters here.
